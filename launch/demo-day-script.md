# Demo day (3 minutes, draft)

1. **0:00 Problem (20s).** Every team here needed a demo video by the same deadline. Recording, narrating and editing takes hours, and a screen recording still doesn't explain *why* your project matters.
2. **0:20 Live run (90s).** Paste another team's live URL (ask them first) → show the progress stepper and the agent's log lines → play the finished video.
   - Backup: if Wi-Fi or the site misbehaves, open a finished job page and play that video.
3. **1:50 Revise (30s).** Type "make it shorter" → show it continues the same HarnessRouter session.
4. **2:20 Numbers (30s).** Open `/stats`: videos made, teams, finalists who used Pit Crew, success rate, median time. Read one consented quote.
5. **2:50 Close (10s).** Others record a walkthrough; Pit Crew makes your pitch. Next: pitch videos for every launch: Product Hunt, YC demo days, investor updates.
